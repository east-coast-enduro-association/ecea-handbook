# Google Workspace

We use Google Workspace for email, documents, and file storage.

## Who Gets an @ecea.org Account?

Board members and key volunteers receive organizational email accounts (e.g., `president@ecea.org`, `treasurer@ecea.org`).

## Accessing Shared Documents

### If you have an @ecea.org account:
1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)
2. Sign in with your @ecea.org email
3. Look for "Shared drives" in the left sidebar

### If you don't have an @ecea.org account:
You can still access documents shared with you:
1. Click the link in the email/chat message
2. Sign in with your personal Google account (or any email)
3. You may receive a PIN code via email to verify your identity

## Shared Drive Structure

```
ECEA Shared Drive/
├── Meeting Minutes/
│   ├── 2024/
│   └── 2025/
├── Administrative/
│   ├── Bylaws/
│   └── Policies/
├── Legal/
└── Projects/
```

## Tips

- **Don't move files** from shared folders without asking - others may have links to them
- **Use Google Docs** for documents that need collaboration (not Word files)
- **Share links**, not attachments, when sending documents via email or chat

## Getting Help

Contact the Technology Chair (see [Contacts](contacts.md)) if you:
- Can't access a document
- Need a new shared folder created
- Have questions about organizing files
