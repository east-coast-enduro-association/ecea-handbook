# Zulip Chat

Zulip is our team chat platform. Unlike email, it's designed for ongoing conversations and quick discussions between meetings.

## Why Zulip?

- **Nothing gets lost** - Unlike Facebook or Slack, conversations are organized by topic
- **Catch up easily** - You can see what topics had activity while you were away
- **Searchable** - Find any past discussion quickly

## Key Concepts

### Streams
Streams are like channels or categories. Examples:
- **#general** - Announcements and general discussion
- **#board** - Board member discussions
- **#events** - Event planning
- **#introductions** - New member welcomes

### Topics
Every message in a stream has a topic (like an email subject line). This keeps conversations organized.

Example in **#events** stream:
- Topic: "Spring Fundraiser 2025"
- Topic: "Volunteer Appreciation Night"
- Topic: "Monthly Meetup Schedule"

## How to Use Zulip

### Posting a Message
1. Select a stream from the left sidebar
2. Enter a topic (be descriptive!)
3. Type your message and press Enter

### Catching Up
1. Click "All messages" to see recent activity
2. Or click a specific stream to see just that area
3. Unread topics are highlighted

### Notifications
- Desktop: Click the gear icon > Notifications
- Mobile: Check app settings
- You can mute streams you don't need to follow closely

## Etiquette

- **Use descriptive topics** - "Question about bylaws" not "Quick question"
- **Stay on topic** - Start a new topic for new subjects
- **@mention sparingly** - Use @name only when you need someone's attention
- **Be patient** - Not everyone checks chat constantly
