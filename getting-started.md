# Getting Started

Welcome to ECEA! This guide will help you get set up with our technology systems.

## Step 1: Google Workspace Account

If you're a board member, you'll receive an @ecea.org email account.

1. You'll receive an invitation email at your personal address
2. Click the link to set up your password
3. Sign in at [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com) with your new @ecea.org email

Your account gives you access to:
- ECEA email
- Google Drive (shared documents)
- Google Calendar (meeting invites)

**Not a board member?** You can still access shared documents using your personal Google account. See [Google Workspace](google-workspace.md) for details.

## Step 2: Join Zulip (Chat)

Zulip is where we communicate between meetings.

1. You'll receive an invitation email
2. Create your account
3. Download the app (optional but recommended):
   - [Desktop app](https://zulip.com/apps/)
   - [Mobile app](https://zulip.com/apps/) (iOS and Android)

See [Zulip Guide](zulip.md) for how we use chat.

## Step 3: Introduce Yourself

Once you're in Zulip, head to the **#introductions** stream and say hello!
